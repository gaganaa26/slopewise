7. Scientific limitations

  • LOS displacement is the 1-D projection of 3-D ground motion onto the radar line of sight and is relative to a stable reference level, not absolute.
  • Phase-to-displacement uses d = -λ·Δφ/4π with L-band λ ≈ 0.238 m; the product sign convention is being verified, so direction (toward/away) is not asserted.
  • Low-coherence pixels are excluded and shown grey. Absence of a signal there is NOT evidence of stability.
  • Single 12-day pairs retain tropospheric noise (the ionospheric screen is subtracted where available); the local noise floor is estimated robustly (MAD) from the scene.
  • Product grid is ~80 m: small or fast failures are not resolved.
  • Radar shadow/layover masking and pixel-offset cross-check are NOT applied in this build.
  • The priority index P is a transparent, expert-weighted, UNCALIBRATED relative ranking (not a landslide probability). Missing components are excluded and the remaining weights renormalised.
  • OpenStreetMap completeness varies; rainfall and terrain are included only when supplied (see "not available" markers).
  • Observed deformation and exposure only: SLOPEWISE does not predict the occurrence or date of any landslide.
